Design rules

The locks.

📏Height, alwaysHer head reaches his lower chest. He bends to hear her; she looks up steeply. Never draw them equal.
🪶Faces frozenFace, hair length and skin tone never change. Outfits change with the season.
💎Plain silver onlySimple drop earrings and a fine pendant. No bindi, no ornate ethnic jewellery.
🐾One UI everywhereCoral gradient paw logo, white cards, coral outgoing bubbles — identical on every page.
🧵The coral threadA thin glowing line across the gutter whenever they're in the same scene.
🌸Season paletteEvery chapter opens with a colour bar and a date caption box.
