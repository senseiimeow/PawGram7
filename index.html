The story

Two strangers,
one long thread.

September 5 → April 4. Exactly seven months of typing: late-summer humidity, maple autumn, a winter that tries very hard to break them, and a spring that finally keeps its promise. Nothing here is loud. Everything here is felt.

Told in Korean-webtoon page turns with a recurring motif — a thin coral thread that crosses the gutters between his panel and hers, so you can always see the connection, even when they can't.
